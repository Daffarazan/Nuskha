OPEN NUSKHA ON YOUR PHONE

1. Connect your phone and laptop to the same trusted Wi-Fi network.
2. Double-click START-PHONE.cmd on the laptop (Python is already installed here).
3. Under Wi-Fi in the displayed network details, find the IPv4 Address.
4. In your phone browser, enter http:// followed by that address and :8001
   Example: http://192.168.1.10:8001
5. Tap Buka PDF and choose a PDF on your phone.

Keep the laptop awake and the server window open. Press Ctrl+C to stop.
If the address does not load, check Windows Firewall and Wi-Fi isolation.
University/guest Wi-Fi may block devices from reaching each other; use a
trusted home network or a phone hotspot with the laptop connected instead.
The address can change after reconnecting Wi-Fi.

This server shares only the nuskha-offline app folder with your network.
Selected PDFs stay on the phone. Do not put private files in the app folder.

INSTALL AND USE WITHOUT THE LAPTOP

The Wi-Fi HTTP address does not support offline installation. For that,
serve the app folder at an HTTPS address. See nuskha-offline/BACA-SAYA.txt
for the existing hosting and Android/iPhone installation instructions.
